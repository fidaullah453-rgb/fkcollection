# Google par top aane ke steps (SEO)

1. Site ko apne domain par deploy karein (Vercel) aur setting me `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://aapka-domain.com` add karein.
2. Google Search Console me domain add karein, phir `https://aapka-domain.com/sitemap.xml` submit karein.
3. Google Business Profile banayein (Swat ka address + phone) - local search ke liye sab se zaroori.
4. Apni asal watches ki saaf photos lagayein (Unsplash photos ki jagah) aur har product ki description unique likhein.
5. Asal customers ke reviews + photos add karein. Fake reviews na lagayein.
6. Instagram/Facebook/TikTok se site ka link share karein; backlinks ranking barhate hain.
7. Site tez rakhein: images chhoti (WebP, 200KB se kam) rakhein.
